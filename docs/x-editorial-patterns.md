# X editorial patterns and evidence

Research checked on 2026-11-24 using public, read-only web access. No login,
posting or verification bypass. This is a reference library for experiments,
not a list of proven viral formulas. Historical examples are never used as
today's news inputs.

## Evidence levels

- **A — directly observed:** original post body and labeled metrics readable in
  this research session. No example below reached this level.
- **B — X-domain index snapshot:** the search service returned an `x.com` post
  body, absolute date and explicitly labeled Views. Several originals returned
  403 when opened. Counts are historical indexed snapshots, not current metrics.
- **C — public embedding or mirror:** an accessible page quotes/embeds the post
  and links its original URL. This verifies what that page displayed; original
  availability, screenshot provenance and interaction counts remain unverified.

Views do not establish unique readers, organic distribution, follower growth or
conversion. Follower totals, search interest and a publisher calling something
“viral” are not post-level performance evidence. Unlabeled number sequences were
not interpreted as likes, reposts or bookmarks.

## Official guidance and its scope

[X's introductory guidance](https://business.x.com/en/basics/get-your-business-started-with-x)
supports concise, natural, conversational writing.
[The organic presence guide](https://business.x.com/content/dam/business-twitter/resources/x-downloadable-guides/build-your-presence-on-x-one-sheet.pdf)
recommends subjects readers care about and relevant photos/video. Neither proves a
four-image edition will outperform other formats.

[X's posting help](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/how-to-post) allows four media
items. [ALT help](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/picture-descriptions) and
[description guidance](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/write-image-descriptions)
allow a separate description of up to 1,000 characters per image and recommend
objective descriptions that capture important image text.

[X's creative best practices](https://business.x.com/en/advertising/creative-best-practices)
offer useful mobile readability and image/copy alignment ideas. The page concerns
**advertising**: its performance statistics and 50–100-character recommendation
are not organic algorithm promises. [X's curation style guide](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/curationstyleguide)
also distinguishes a repeated shared meme from an isolated joke and warns about
misleading context.

## Six source-grounded candidate patterns

Brackets are evidence slots, not permission to invent material. English supplies
the context an unfamiliar reader needs; Chinese can concentrate on the local
meaning and concrete detail. Facts and uncertainty must agree across languages.

| Pattern | 中文模板 | English template | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `specific_observation` | 今天在[平台/场景]看到一个细节：[可见行为]。它与[已知用途/变化]有关。 | One detail from [platform/place] today: [observable behavior]. It connects to [documented use/change]. | Specific saved fact; no “China is obsessed” extrapolation. |
| `source_quote` | 在[日期/原帖]下，一位用户写道：“[原文]”。这里的[词]指[语境]。 | “[Faithful translation],” wrote one user under [specific post/date]. Here, [term] means [context]. | Actual saved, attributed quotation and source URL. No invented opposing quote. |
| `contextual_number` | [数字+单位]是[来源]对[对象/时期]的统计。比较基准是[分母/基准]；限制是[条件]。 | [Number + unit] is [source]'s figure for [population/period]. Compare it with [baseline]. One caveat: [condition]. | Number, definition, period and baseline. Business growth is not post impact. |
| `counterpoint` | [具名来源]用[解释A]说明[现象]。[原始证据]提示，还要看[机制B]。 | [Named source] frames [event] as [explanation A]. [Primary evidence] points to another factor: [mechanism B]. | A real attributable claim and supporting evidence; never invent “everyone thinks.” |
| `culture_translation` | “[词语]”这次出现在[具体场景]。字面是[字面义]，这里更接近[语用含义]。 | “[Phrase]” literally means [literal meaning]. In this [specific context], it works more like [functional equivalent]. | Documented wording and context. An isolated phrase is not automatically a nationwide meme. |
| `update` | 昨天[有日期的事实]。今天的新进展是[新事实+来源]。[未知事项]仍待确认。 | Yesterday, [dated fact]. Today's update: [new sourced fact]. We still don't know [unresolved point]. | Both dated facts and a real new development; no stale post disguised as an update. |

## Reach mechanisms to observe, not assume

These are editorial hypotheses inferred from the forms above. The accessible
evidence does not establish why anyone saw, shared or followed an account.

- **Recognition followed by a new detail:** AitaiKuji names a recognizable fandom
  object, then adds a specific creator and unfinished design process. Test whether
  that concrete entry point attracts relevant readers rather than broad curiosity.
- **A phrase that travels:** the Chinglish example lets readers recognize a social
  function before learning an unfamiliar idiom. Preserve the real phrase and its
  context; do not manufacture an entertaining translation or imply consensus.
- **A checkable explanation:** the technical examples link a named argument or
  measurement to a mechanism and its conditions. Test whether that creates useful
  replies or saves; neither follows nor trust can be inferred from raw views.
- **A reason to return:** Duolingo's next-day chart advances its earlier observation
  with a new company metric. Test genuinely new evidence as a follow-up; do not
  imply the earlier post caused the reported business change.
- **A meaningful conversation:** end with one unresolved, evidence-related question
  when a story has one. [X's community guidance](https://business.x.com/en/basics/community-management)
  encourages real replies and conversation; it does not guarantee distribution.
  Avoid empty demands to comment, invented controversy and compulsory two-sided framing.

For review, record which reader need a candidate serves, the evidence supporting
its hook, and what outcome could test that hypothesis. Views indicate exposure
only; relevant replies, profile visits and follows answer different questions and
require the account's own observed analytics.

## Applying this in WcCAT

- Daily English edition: complete Top10 ranking overview plus up to three
  source-supported detail cards. Three usable stories produce four images; sparse
  evidence never pads the detail cards or removes other headlines from the overview.
  The overview marks selected stories while preserving the complete original rank
  order; source links remain traceable in the saved data/export.
  Generation assigns this layout on the server even if the model omits its flag;
  already-saved editions without `layout: 'editorial'` retain their legacy contract.
- Independent story angles use **Inside China's Internet**, **In Their Words**
  and **China Tech, in Context**. The daily package may cover several unrelated
  subjects. It need not fill every column or force every story into a debate.
- Single-story selection uses today/yesterday in the publishing timezone,
  separately localized EN/ZH copy and different supported hook patterns. No read,
  preview or export invents new research.
- Preserve real comment URLs, original words, source like counts and saved
  translations. Emotion scores remain labeled AI interpretation, not a poll.
- Compare candidate angles as editorial experiments. If account analytics later
  becomes available, record observation time and 24h/72h outcomes separately.
  Missing data stays unknown; cross-account historical views do not establish a
  causal hook formula.
